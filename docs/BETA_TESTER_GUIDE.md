# Ahava beta: tester guide

Thanks for testing Ahava. This is an early test version. It is **not for emergencies and not medical advice**. In an emergency call **112** or **10177**. Please use **demo or low-sensitivity health data** while we finish hardening it.

## 1. Join

1. Open the link we sent you (it has your invite code in it).
2. Create your account. If you open the site without the link, type your invite code in the "Beta invite code" box.
3. Complete your profile when asked.

## 2. Try Symptom Check

Open **Symptom Check** and describe a made-up or mild symptom. You get a suggested urgency and next step. A clinician reviews results, so allow time. Tell us if anything was confusing or wrong.

## 3. Log your vitals (the main test)

Ahava learns **your** normal from your readings. It needs about **14 days**, so for the first two weeks it will say it is still calibrating, and personal alerts only start after that. Before then it only checks readings against safe limits.

To get useful data, log **twice a day** (for example, when you wake up and in the evening), at the same times each day:

1. Sit and rest for **5 minutes**. No exercise, coffee or smoking in the 30 minutes before.
2. On the dashboard, tap **Log today's vitals**.
3. Enter only what you actually measured:

| Field | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Resting heart rate | Your watch, while seated and rested |
| Oxygen saturation | Watch spot-check (rough, not clinical grade) or a finger oximeter |
| Blood pressure | An **upper-arm cuff only**. Watches are not reliable for this |
| Temperature | A thermometer |
| HRV, RMSSD (ms) | Garmin, Fitbit or Samsung. **Apple Watch: leave blank** (it shows a different measure) |
| Breathing rate | Your watch, if it shows it |

Leave a field empty if you did not measure it. Please do not guess or round numbers.

## 4. Tell us what happened

Send us:
- Anything confusing, slow or broken, with a screenshot if you can.
- Whether the daily logging felt like too much, and what would make it easier.
- Any alert you got, and whether it seemed reasonable.

## What is not in the beta yet

- Automatic syncing from your watch or phone (Health Connect, Apple Health). For now you type readings in.
- Background reminders to log.

Your account and data can be deleted on request: tell us and we will do it.
